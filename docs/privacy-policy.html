Privacy at a glance

You choose your photos

We use Apple's system photo picker and receive only the images you explicitly select. We do not automatically browse your entire photo library.

AI processing requires uploads

Your selected images, prompts, and generation settings are sent to servers to perform restoration, enhancement, image generation, or video generation.

Tracking is your choice

Where required, cross-app or cross-website advertising measurement is subject to the iOS tracking permission. Refusing it does not disable core editing features.

You can manage your data

You can delete individual generation records, withdraw device permissions, and request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Photo Revival mobile application (the “App”) and services directly associated with the App. In this Policy, “Photo Revival,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the provider of the App. For privacy questions, contact berkehancanat@gmail.com.

Apple, Google, Adjust, and other third parties may independently determine how they process information through their services. Their activities are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Device Permissions

  • Photos: Used to select source images or a feedback screenshot. The App uses the system photo picker, and only items you explicitly select are provided to the App.
  • Tracking: Used, where applicable, for advertising measurement across apps or websites. You may refuse this permission without losing access to core editing and generation features.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver service messages you permit and may be part of a reward task. You can refuse or disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings under “Privacy & Security” or on the App's system settings page. Withdrawing permission does not affect processing lawfully completed beforehand.

We use information as necessary to provide and protect the service, including to:

  1. Create and manage accounts, complete Apple or Google sign-in, and maintain sessions;
  2. Receive source material, prompts, and settings and create, display, and delete AI generation tasks;
  3. Verify App Store subscriptions, restore purchases, and manage memberships, credits, and transaction records;
  4. Provide check-in, reward, referral, and anti-abuse functions;
  5. Respond to feedback, diagnose errors, secure servers and accounts, and prevent fraud or misuse;
  6. Measure installation sources and product performance and improve features and user experience; and
  7. Comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We will not use personal information for a materially incompatible new purpose without appropriate notice and any consent required by law.

6. Advertising Attribution and Tracking

The App integrates the Adjust SDK to understand installation sources and measure advertising campaigns. Adjust may process certain device and attribution information without IDFA, including IDFV, Adjust ADID, device or operating-system information, IP address, referral data, and App events.

When processing constitutes “tracking” as defined by Apple, the App first presents the iOS App Tracking Transparency request. Only after you authorize tracking may the relevant SDK access IDFA and conduct authorized cross-app or cross-website advertising measurement. You can change your choice under “Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.” Refusing or withdrawing tracking permission does not affect core service functionality.

7. AI Content Processing

  • AI features require us to send your selected images, prompts, template identifier, and output settings to servers and relevant AI processing providers.
  • Results are generated automatically and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected. Do not rely on them as the sole basis for medical, legal, identity-verification, or other high-risk decisions.
  • You must have lawful authority to upload the content and obtain any required permission from identifiable people depicted in it.
  • We do not publicly display or sell your private uploads. We will not use your private photos to train a general-purpose AI model for other customers unless we separately provide clear notice and obtain any consent required by law.

8. Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and protect the service, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations:

  • Account and membership data: Generally retained until your account is deleted, followed by any limited period necessary and permitted by law.
  • Uploads, results, and generation history: Stored with your account so you can access history until you delete the relevant task or request deletion of your account. Temporary processing copies are removed when no longer needed to complete or secure the requested task.
  • Deleted information and backups: Information removed from active systems may remain temporarily in isolated backups. It is not used for ordinary business and is removed through the normal backup-rotation process unless longer retention is required by law.
  • Feedback: Retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, improve the service, handle related disputes, or comply with legal obligations.
  • Transaction, log, and security records: Retained as needed for tax, accounting, anti-fraud, dispute-resolution, and other legal requirements.
  • Local data: Generally remains on your device until you sign out, clear system data, or delete the App.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it unless continued retention is legally required.

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Withdraw consent, object to or restrict certain processing, and request data portability where applicable;

Deleting data or your account

  1. You can delete an individual generation record using the delete control on the App's history page.
  2. To request complete deletion of your account and associated information, email berkehancanat@gmail.com. We may reasonably verify account ownership before completing a request to prevent unauthorized deletion.
Deleting the App, signing out, or disabling Apple or Google sign-in does not automatically delete your server account. Deleting your Photo Revival account also does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. You must separately manage auto-renewal in your Apple ID subscription settings.

We respond to verifiable requests within the period required by applicable law. Where permitted, we may refuse a manifestly unfounded or excessive request, a request that would impair another person's rights, or one that conflicts with a legal obligation, and we will explain the reason.
