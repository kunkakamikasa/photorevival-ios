1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Photo Revival mobile application (the “App”) and services directly associated with the App. In this Policy, “Photo Revival,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the provider of the App. For privacy questions, contact berkehancanat@gmail.com.

Apple, Google (including Firebase and AdMob), Adjust, Meta, and other third parties may independently determine how they process information through their services. Their activities are governed by their own privacy policies.

  • Photos and media: Used to select source images or a feedback screenshot and to save results at your request. 6. Analytics, Advertising Attribution, and Tracking

Analytics, advertising, attribution, and tracking SDK availability can differ by platform and App version. The Android version's Google Play disclosure describes the SDKs present in that specific Android release. The following describes processing when the relevant integration is enabled.

Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to measure product usage and purchase performance. Firebase may assign an app-instance or installation identifier and receive App and device information together with the events described in Section 2. When you are signed in, we provide our internal account ID as the Firebase Analytics user ID and provide authentication and subscription status as user properties. This allows analytics events to be associated with the same App account. Firebase Analytics collection is used for product analytics even when the activity does not constitute cross-app tracking under Apple's definition.

Adjust

The App integrates the Adjust SDK to understand installation sources and measure advertising campaigns. Adjust may process certain device and attribution information without IDFA, including IDFV, Adjust ADID, device or operating-system information, IP address, referral data, and App events.

Meta / Facebook SDK

The App initializes the Meta/Facebook SDK to support advertising attribution and measurement. Depending on SDK configuration, your device settings, and Meta's services, it may automatically record App activation or purchase-related events and process device identifiers and limited SDK diagnostic information. Meta may use permitted data for analytics, App functionality, and advertising measurement under its own privacy policy.

Google AdMob and advertising consent

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display ads. The SDK may process IP address to estimate general location, device identifiers, information about ads requested or shown, ad and product interactions, and limited crash, performance, or diagnostic data. This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, control frequency, improve advertising performance, maintain security, and prevent invalid activity. Advertising providers participating in an AdMob ad request may process permitted information under their own privacy policies.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents an advertising privacy message and records your selections before the App requests ads. Depending on applicable law and your selections, you may receive personalized, non-personalized, limited, or technically delivered ads. When Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, you can reopen the form from “Settings > Privacy Choices” and change your selection. Withdrawing a choice does not affect processing lawfully completed beforehand.

iOS tracking permission

When processing constitutes “tracking” as defined by Apple, the App first presents the iOS App Tracking Transparency request. Only after you authorize tracking may AdMob, Adjust, Meta, Firebase, or another relevant SDK access IDFA or conduct authorized cross-app or cross-website advertising measurement. You can change your choice under “Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.” Refusing or withdrawing tracking permission does not affect core service functionality. It may not stop first-party product analytics that does not use IDFA and is not considered tracking by Apple, and it does not necessarily prevent non-personalized, limited, or technically delivered ads.

7. AI Content Processing

  • AI features require us to send your selected images, prompts, template identifier, and output settings to servers and relevant AI processing providers.
  • Results are generated automatically and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected. Do not rely on them as the sole basis for medical, legal, identity-verification, or other high-risk decisions.
  • You must have lawful authority to upload the content and obtain any required permission from identifiable people depicted in it.
  • You may use the in-App Report action on a generated result or creation-history item to flag offensive, unsafe, or otherwise inappropriate AI output for review. The report is linked to the relevant generation task so we can investigate and improve safeguards.
  • We do not publicly display or sell your private uploads. We will not use your private photos to train a general-purpose AI model for other customers unless we separately provide clear notice and obtain any consent required by law.

8. Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and protect the service, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations:

  • Account and membership data: Generally retained until your account is deleted, followed by any limited period necessary and permitted by law.
  • Uploads, results, and generation history: Stored with your account so you can access history until you delete the relevant task or request deletion of your account. Temporary processing copies are removed when no longer needed to complete or secure the requested task.
  • Deleted information and backups: Information removed from active systems may remain temporarily in isolated backups. It is not used for ordinary business and is removed through the normal backup-rotation process unless longer retention is required by law.
  • Feedback: Retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, improve the service, handle related disputes, or comply with legal obligations.
  • Transaction, log, and security records: Retained as needed for tax, accounting, anti-fraud, dispute-resolution, and other legal requirements.
  • Analytics, attribution, and advertising data: Retained for the periods configured in Firebase, AdMob, Adjust, and Meta and only as long as reasonably necessary for product analytics, ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Aggregated or anonymized statistics may be retained longer.
  • Local data: Generally remains on your device until you sign out, clear system data, or delete the App.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it unless continued retention is legally required.

10. Delete Your Account

You can delete your Photo Revival account directly in the App:

  1. Open Me and select the settings button.
  2. Open your profile card, then select Delete Account.
  3. Review the warning and select Confirm.

If you cannot access the App, email berkehancanat@gmail.com with the subject “Photo Revival Account Deletion.” We may reasonably verify account ownership before completing a request so that another person cannot delete your account.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your authentication account, profile, uploaded source files, generated content and task history, credit wallet, rewards and referral records, feedback linked to your account, and other user records that are configured to be deleted with the account.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited deletion audit record and transaction, refund, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, and dispute records for the periods required or permitted by law. Information may also remain temporarily in isolated backups until normal backup rotation completes. Retained records are not used to recreate your account or for ordinary product use.

Deleting the App, signing out, or disabling Apple or Google sign-in does not automatically delete your server account. Deleting your Photo Revival account also does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. You must separately manage auto-renewal in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

We respond to verifiable requests within the period required by applicable law. Where permitted, we may refuse a manifestly unfounded or excessive request, a request that would impair another person's rights, or one that conflicts with a legal obligation, and we will explain the reason.
